Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with SASS BOTTLE

What areas in Kampala do you deliver to? +

We currently deliver to Kansanga, Muyenga, Nakawa, Kampala Central, and surrounding areas. Delivery fees vary by zone and are shown clearly at checkout before you confirm your order.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept Cash on Delivery and Mobile Money. At checkout, selecting Mobile Money opens your phone's payment app directly with the amount pre-filled, so paying takes just a few taps.

Do you verify age before delivery? +

Yes. By browsing our site you confirm you are of legal drinking age, and our delivery team may ask for ID on arrival for alcoholic orders. SASS BOTTLE promotes responsible consumption.

How long does delivery take? +

Most orders within Kampala are delivered the same day, typically within 1–3 hours depending on your zone and the time you place your order.

Can I track my order after placing it? +

Absolutely. After checkout you'll see an order confirmation screen, and you can visit your account's "My Orders" page at any time to check the current status of your delivery.

What if an item arrives damaged or incorrect? +

Contact us immediately through our Contact page with your order details. We'll arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

Do you cater for events or bulk orders? +

Yes! For parties, weddings, or corporate events, reach out through our Contact page with your requirements and we'll put together a tailored order for you.

What are your opening hours? +

We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Orders placed outside these hours will be processed first thing the next business day.
