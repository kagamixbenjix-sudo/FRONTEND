The Heart Behind SASS BOTTLE

SASS BOTTLE was born from a simple idea — that great beverages should be easy to find, beautifully presented, and delivered right to your door. Nestled in the heart of Kansanga, Kampala, our boutique brings together a carefully curated selection of spirits, wines, beers, and non-alcoholic drinks for every occasion.

We believe that every bottle tells a story — of a celebration, a quiet evening, a gathering of friends. Our mission is to be part of those moments, making premium beverages accessible to everyone in Kampala.

From imported whiskies and champagnes to local favourites and refreshing sodas, our collection is built with care and love for our community. Whether you're shopping for a special occasion or just stocking up, SASS BOTTLE is here for you.
